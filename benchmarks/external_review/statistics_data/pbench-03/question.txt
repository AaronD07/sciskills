Research Question:
Does the Promise 1 treatment effect on city-council vote-switching differ from its effect on mayoral vote-switching?

Hypothesis:
H0: The Promise 1 coefficient is equal in the city-council and mayoral vote-switching equations.
H1: The Promise 1 coefficient differs between the city-council and mayoral vote-switching equations.

STUDY DESIGN:
- Randomized field experiment conducted among individual voters in Sorsogon City, Philippines, during the 2013 elections.
- Unit of observation: Individual voter.
- Study arms include a control condition and two promise treatments.
- The task compares vote-switching responses across city council, mayor, and vice-mayor races.

VARIABLE GLOSSARY:
- cc_switched: City-council vote-switching indicator (binary; missing when not measured) [OUTCOME]
- m_switched: Mayoral vote-switching indicator (binary; missing when not measured) [OUTCOME]
- vm_switched: Vice-mayoral vote-switching indicator (binary; missing when not measured)
- pt2: Promise 1 treatment indicator [TREATMENT]
- pt3: Promise 2 treatment indicator [TREATMENT]
- respondent_id: Voter identifier; a voter can contribute an observation to each race's equation
- marital_stat: Marital status (categorical)
- male, age, catholic, hh_voting_mems: Demographic controls
- employ_stat1-employ_stat6: Employment-status indicators
- elementary, some_highschool, highschool, some_college, vocational, college_up: Education indicators
- adultmigrant, childmigrant, bornhere: Migration-history indicators
- promise_treatment: Raw treatment-assignment code
