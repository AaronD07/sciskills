Research Question:
Does gamma interferon treatment reduce the risk of serious infections among patients with chronic granulomatous disease in this placebo-controlled randomized trial?

Hypothesis:
H0: Gamma interferon treatment does not reduce the risk of serious infections compared with placebo.
H1: Gamma interferon treatment reduces the risk of serious infections compared with placebo.

STUDY DESIGN:
- Type: Placebo-controlled randomized clinical trial
- Unit of observation: Infection-risk interval recorded for a CGD patient
- Cohort: 203 observations from 128 patients with chronic granulomatous disease
- Comparison: Gamma interferon (`treat = rIFN-g`) versus placebo

VARIABLE GLOSSARY:
- status: Indicator for a serious infection event during the interval [OUTCOME]
- treat: Treatment assignment, gamma interferon (`rIFN-g`) versus placebo [TREATMENT]
- tstart: Interval start time for follow-up
- tstop: Interval end time for follow-up
- id: Patient identifier
- enum: Event-order index for repeated intervals within patient
- sex: Patient sex
- age: Patient age
- inherit: Inheritance pattern, X-linked versus autosomal
- steroids: Steroid use indicator
- propylac: Prophylactic antibacterial use indicator
