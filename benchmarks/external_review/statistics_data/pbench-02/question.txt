Research Question:
Do immunisation camps with incentives affect complete immunisation rates among children in neighbouring, non-targeted households in rural Rajasthan, relative to control villages?

Hypothesis:
H0: The camp-plus-incentives coefficient is zero for children in neighbouring, non-targeted households.
H1: The camp-plus-incentives coefficient is nonzero for children in neighbouring, non-targeted households.

STUDY DESIGN:
- Type: Cluster-randomized trial
- Randomization level: Village
- Unit of observation: Child
- Source cohort: Spillover cohort from 134 villages in rural Udaipur district, Rajasthan, India
- Study arms: Control villages, camp-only villages, and camp-plus-incentive villages
- Key restriction: Focus on children in spillover households in treatment villages and children in control villages

VARIABLE GLOSSARY:
- fully_immunized: Completely immunised child indicator (binary: 0/1) [OUTCOME]
- TreatNonIncentive: Village assigned to camp-only intervention (binary: 0/1) [TREATMENT]
- TreatIncentive: Village assigned to camp-plus-incentive intervention (binary: 0/1) [TREATMENT] [TARGET]
- spillover: Child is from a neighbouring, non-targeted household in a treatment village (binary: 0/1) [SAMPLE FILTER]
- NonTreat: Village assigned to control group (binary: 0/1)
- num_vaccines: Total immunisations received (count)
- any_immunized: Child received at least one immunisation (binary: 0/1)
- agemon: Child age in months
- locid: Village identifier
