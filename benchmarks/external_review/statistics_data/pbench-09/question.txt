Research Question:
Is maternal smoking during pregnancy associated with a higher likelihood of low birth weight among women in this birthweight dataset, accounting for key maternal characteristics?

Hypothesis:
H0: Maternal smoking during pregnancy is not associated with a higher likelihood of low birth weight
H1: Maternal smoking during pregnancy is associated with a higher likelihood of low birth weight

STUDY DESIGN:
- Type: Observational cross-sectional birthweight study
- Unit of observation: Woman/pregnancy
- Sample: 189 women
- Source: Medical / Biostatistics benchmark birthweight dataset

VARIABLE GLOSSARY:
- low: Low birth weight indicator (binary: 0/1) [OUTCOME]
- smoke: Maternal smoking during pregnancy (binary: 0/1) [EXPOSURE] [TARGET]
- age: Maternal age (years)
- lwt: Maternal weight
- race: Maternal race category
- bwt: Birth weight
