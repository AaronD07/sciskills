Research Question:
Did the GoBifo community-driven development program improve economic welfare among villages in post-conflict Sierra Leone, as measured by the economic welfare index at endline?

Hypothesis:
H0: The GoBifo program has no association with the economic welfare index at endline.
H1: The GoBifo program is associated with a change in the economic welfare index at endline.

STUDY DESIGN:
- Type: Randomized community-level study
- Unit of observation: Village
- Setting: Post-conflict Sierra Leone communities in the GoBifo study
- Comparison: GoBifo treatment villages vs. control villages
- Timing: Endline cross-section
- Grouping structure: Villages are organized within wards

VARIABLE GLOSSARY:
- zscore_3: Economic welfare index for Hypothesis 3 (continuous) [OUTCOME]
- t: Indicator that the village was assigned to GoBifo (binary: 0/1) [TREATMENT]
- id_vill: Village identifier
- tothhs: Total households in the village
- road: Road access indicator
- ward_1 to ward_20: Ward indicators
