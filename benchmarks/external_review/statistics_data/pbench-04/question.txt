Research Question:
Is patient age associated with three-vessel or left main coronary disease among patients undergoing cardiac catheterization?

Hypothesis:
H0: Patient age is not associated with the presence of three-vessel or left main coronary disease.
H1: Patient age is associated with the presence of three-vessel or left main coronary disease.

STUDY DESIGN:
- Type: Observational cross-sectional cardiac catheterization study
- Unit of observation: Patient
- Source: Vanderbilt University Department of Biostatistics cardiac catheterization diagnostic data
- Sample: 3,504 cardiac catheterization patients

VARIABLE GLOSSARY:
- tvdlm: Three-vessel or left main coronary disease (binary: 0/1) [OUTCOME]
- age: Patient age
- sex: Patient sex
- cad.dur: Duration of coronary artery disease
- choleste: Cholesterol measure
- sigdz: Significant coronary disease indicator (binary: 0/1)
