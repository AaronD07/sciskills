Research Question:
Among wives' individual accounts in the Kenya study, does the total effect of receiving a free ATM card on the standardized savings outcome differ from zero?

Hypothesis:
H0: The total ATM effect for wives' individual accounts is zero (freeatm + atm_wife = 0).
H1: The total ATM effect for wives' individual accounts is nonzero (freeatm + atm_wife != 0).

STUDY DESIGN:
- Type: Randomized controlled trial
- Setting: Married couples in Kenya
- Unit of observation: Account
- Sample: Opened accounts only
- Comparison: Free ATM card assignment, with effects allowed to vary by account type

VARIABLE GLOSSARY:
- avg_eff: Standardized mean effect across 10 savings outcomes (continuous) [OUTCOME]
- freeatm: Assigned to receive a free ATM card (binary: 0/1) [TREATMENT]
- atm_hub: Derived indicator capturing the free-ATM effect for husbands' individual accounts
- atm_wife: Derived indicator capturing the free-ATM effect for wives' individual accounts
- joint: Joint account indicator (binary: 0/1) [TARGET]
- ind_h: Husband's individual account indicator (binary: 0/1) [TARGET]
- ind_w: Wife's individual account indicator (binary: 0/1) [TARGET]
- open: Account opened indicator (binary: 0/1)
