Research Question:
Does seasonal migration increase household savings among rural Bangladeshi households in the 2008 follow-up?

Hypothesis:
H0: Seasonal migration does not increase household savings.
H1: Seasonal migration increases household savings.

STUDY DESIGN:
- Type: Randomized encouragement study of seasonal migration
- Unit of observation: Household
- Source cohort: Rural households in Rangpur, Bangladesh
- Time frame: 2008 follow-up round
- Study arms: Households assigned to cash, credit, or information encouragement conditions or to control
- Key restriction: The analytic sample excludes households with total savings above 20,000 Taka

VARIABLE GLOSSARY:
- tot_save: Total household savings in Taka [OUTCOME]
- migrant_new: Indicator that any household member migrated [EXPOSURE]
- cash: Assigned to cash encouragement condition [TREATMENT]
- credit: Assigned to credit encouragement condition [TREATMENT]
- info: Assigned to information encouragement condition [TREATMENT]
- incentivized: Indicator for receiving a migration encouragement assignment [TARGET]
- upazila: Sub-district identifier
- village: Village identifier
- hhid: Household identifier
