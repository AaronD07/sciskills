# Eight filing investigations

These questions use 22 tables from eight issuer-filed Forms 10-K. Each participant receives the relevant tables, a short paraphrase of the accompanying disclosures, and the task. The JSON answer keys, audit calculations below, and other issuers’ records stay outside the workspace.

The table snapshots preserve the original row order, units, signs, and fiscal periods. Blank layout cells and standalone currency markers are removed, adjoining percent and closing-parenthesis cells are joined, and whitespace within cells is normalized. Some heading and section rows have fewer cells than data rows. The manifest records the EDGAR document URLs, SHA-256 digests, and zero-based HTML table indices. The data are historical filing facts. The prompts and counterfactual assumptions are study materials. [Source manifest](sources.json)

## Independent checks

- **Apple.** Compute the sales bridge twice, once at each year’s geographic margins. Average those results for 3,061.1028. The rest of the 11,156 increase in segment operating income is 8,094.8972. Greater China is the only geography whose sales fell. The R&D increase of 1,455 and other corporate expense increase of 786 reduce the consolidated gain to 8,915. [Segment note and consolidated income](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019324000123/aapl-20240928.htm)
- **Microsoft.** The commitments line explicitly includes interest. Its first year is 12,250 versus 7,435 in Note 14 maturities and 5,929 in current lease balances. The all-years note payments of 55,719 less recognized obligations of 46,222 imply 9,497 interest. The filing gives no category split for the 4,815 first-year excess. Claims A and D hold. [Commitments and Note 14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000095017024087843/msft-20240630.htm)
- **Alphabet.** Noncash compensation of 432 plus the year-end accrual of 77 accounts for all 509 of the difference between expense and cash. The aggregate payment fraction is 1,579 / 1,656, while the R&D severance fraction is 848 / 2,088. The filing does not divide each function’s severance between cash and stock compensation. Claims B and D hold. [Cost actions note](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204424000022/goog-20231231.htm)
- **Netflix.** Tabulated obligations reconcile as 7,044,643 recognized plus 14,668,706 unrecognized, in thousands. Amounts due after three years also follow from 21,713,349 minus the first two time buckets of 10,328,923 and 8,784,302. The illustrative upper scenario adds $4 billion to the disclosed total. Timing of unknown titles within twelve months cannot be established exactly. Claims A and D hold. [Contractual obligations, balance sheet and Note 8](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065280/000106528024000030/nflx-20231231.htm)
- **Southwest.** At 2022’s realized economic cost per gallon, the additional 221 million gallons cost 679.2128. Subtract that from the expense increase of 294 to recover the rate effect. The economic adjustments were −16 in 2023 and −68 in 2022. Their difference of 52 includes 28 from the disappearance of the nondesignated-contract premium benefit. [Fuel reconciliation, operating statistics and derivative note](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/92380/000009238024000027/luv-20231231.htm)
- **NVIDIA.** Average recorded inventory is 5,220.5. Reversing the closing provision increases average inventory by 387 and reduces cost of revenue to 15,847. Multiply the resulting inventory-to-cost ratios by 364. Gross profit rises from 44,301 to 45,075 on unchanged revenue of 60,922. Work in process grew from 466 to 1,505. [Income statement and Note 10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581024000029/nvda-20240128.htm)
- **Amazon.** Subtract other lease principal of 683 from 4,384 to recover 3,701 of equipment principal. Replacing it with 310 of equipment additions produces a 3,391 spread. The same substitution for 2022 gives 7,941 − 670 − 299 = 6,972. The spread narrowed by 3,581. The shared financing-obligation deduction cancels. Claims B and C hold. [Cash flow reconciliations and supplemental table](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872424000008/amzn-20231231.htm)
- **Ford.** End-2023 pension assets total 54,381 against obligations of 56,680. End-2022 assets of 54,266 against obligations of 54,472 imply a starting deficit of 206. Translation increases assets by 990 and obligations by 960. Amendments add 627 to obligations. Removing both effects changes the actual deterioration of 2,093 into a deterioration of 1,496. [Management summary and Note 17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/37996/000003799624000009/f-20231231.htm)
