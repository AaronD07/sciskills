# Audit a scientific claim

1. State the decision this deliverable supports. In the language of the assignment, name its unit, intended population and the evidence required to make it.
2. Derive what you should expect from the original request and governing sources. Work outward from the original records to determine what actually happened. Two summaries produced by the same procedure can share a mistake.
3. Check a case that could change the decision. If the evidence disagrees, locate the first discrepancy and see whether you can resolve it with the supplied authority, data and tools.
4. Deliver the result the evidence supports. Preserve valid work, correct or narrow unsupported claims, and identify any material gap that remains. Check the saved artifact and its conclusions together.
