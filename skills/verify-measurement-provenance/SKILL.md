# Verify measurement provenance

1. Translate the requested result into a quantity, unit, observation period, population and measurement method. Read the source definitions and the policy that governs use of the records.
2. Follow the reported value back to individual observations. Establish which records are valid at the decision date and quantify coverage. Identify what a secondary source measures before treating it as evidence for the same quantity.
3. Recompute the supported result. Keep observations and authorized estimates distinguishable. If a quantity cannot be determined, identify the missing evidence and complete the parts the data support.
4. Revisit each recommendation that relied on the measurement. Make the saved decision consistent with the supported quantity, coverage and uncertainty.
